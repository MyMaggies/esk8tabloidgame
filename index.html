The press code

You are a reporter for the Gulch Gazette. Each case is a place to explore, people to talk to, and a headline to write. Here is how it works.

1. Explore

Walk with the arrows. Tap the pink A button to talk to people or look at things. Anything with a yellow ! above it has something to say. Watch out for loose flamingos and shiny mud. Lose all your cred (the hearts) and you wake up at the entrance, minus a point penalty and a note.

2. Judge

People will tell you things. Some are facts, some are guesses, some are exaggerations, and some are just boring. You decide what goes in your notebook.

3. Write

File your story to your editor. Your Leads meter fills as you cover people and places. When you have enough, Mags rings you. Then build a tabloid headline from your notebook, using the language device she asks for. Cover every lead for a bonus.

4. Spot the spin

Answer two quick questions about headline devices. Then see your headline on the front page.

What I expect from a reporter
  • Work on your own device and your own story. Quiet chat is fine, spoiling someone's case is not.
  • Take your time. There are no lives and no timers, but aim for about 8 minutes a case.
  • Keep the sound low, or switch it off with the Sound button.
  • Be professional. A good reporter checks who is speaking and whether they really know.
  • Do not refresh the page. Your notebook and front page live on the screen.
Hazards

Normal has 3 hearts. Gentle has 5 hearts and slower flamingos.

Six cases. Pick any you like. Stars show how tricky the language device is, so start with the easy ones and work up.
